How should each post appear on the home page?

Each card shows category icon, title, subtitle and the first paragraph, with the banner image built in. Click the one that feels right (you can pick more than one if you like a mix).

A

Overlay

Banner fills the top; title and subtitle sit on the image over a dark fade. Most magazine-like and visual, but needs good images.

B

Side by side

Image on the left, text on the right. Compact and scannable; lets more posts fit on a screen. Stacks image-on-top on phones.

C

Overlapping panel

Banner on top, with the text panel lifted over its bottom edge. Image and text feel joined; title stays readable whatever the image.

D

Featured + list

Newest post as a big Overlay (A); everything older as compact Side by side (B). Classic blog front page.
